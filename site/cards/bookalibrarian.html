Book-a-Librarian: a free one-on-one appointment with an expert

Thirty to sixty uninterrupted minutes with a real person who can help.

What it is

  • Book-a-Librarian is a service many libraries offer where you reserve time (typically 30–60 minutes) with a librarian for personalized help: computer or tablet basics, setting up e-book apps, research instruction, genealogy digging, job-search questions, and more.
  • Examples: Hussey-Mayfield Whitestown (Indiana) Public Library offers daily one-on-one appointments on devices, Microsoft apps, e-books, and the library catalog; Valparaiso's and Carmel Clay's Indiana systems run the same service for cardholders; Tippecanoe County Public Library's form even lists 'Resume Help' and 'Job Search Help' as options.
  • Librarians point you to the right sources and teach you the skill — they do not give medical, legal, tax, or business advice, and they will not do your typing or troubleshooting of personal devices.

Cost

  • Free — it is a core library service.
  • Some libraries may ask you to be a cardholder for in-depth sessions; most will help walk-ins too.

What you need to sign up

  • Book through your library's website (search '[your library] book a librarian'), by calling the reference desk, or in person.
  • Tell them the topic ahead of time — 'I want to set up Libby' or 'Help me research my family history' — so they can prep.
  • Bring your device and your library card. Many libraries ask for 1–2 business days to schedule.

Good to know: This is the single best way to learn any of the other items in this pack — bring your card and ask a librarian to walk you through Libby, hoopla, museum passes, or the seed library in one sitting. If your library doesn't use the exact name, ask for 'one-on-one tech help' or 'a research appointment' — it's the same thing.

Real examples you can copy

  • Hussey-Mayfield Memorial Public Library (IN) — Book A Librarian — Indianapolis-area example: request form, topics covered, and limits.
  • Tippecanoe County Public Library (IN) — Form-based booking with topics including resume and job-search help.
  • Evansville Vanderburgh Public Library (IN) — Online, phone, and in-person appointment options.
