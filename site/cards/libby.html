Libby: free e-books and audiobooks on your phone, tablet, or Kindle

The friendly app that turns your library card into an e-reader.

What it is

  • Libby is the free reading app by OverDrive. Thousands of public libraries use it to lend e-books and audiobooks; you sign in with your library card and can borrow instantly instead of waiting for a hold, or place front-of-line holds in some systems.
  • What you can get: bestsellers, classics, business books, kids' titles, magazines, and read-along audiobooks, all free and all legal.
  • Read on phones, tablets, e-readers, and computers. Works offline once downloaded.

Cost

  • Free. The app is free, borrowing is free, and every e-book or audiobook you borrow is free.
  • Optional: Kindle-readers can send compatible Libby loans to a Kindle device or the Kindle app at no cost.

What you need to sign up

  • Phone, tablet, or computer.
  • A valid library card from a participating public library system (the app walks you through finding your library from a list).
  • Search for 'Libby' in your app store, or just visit libbyapp.com in a browser and follow the three-screen setup.

Good to know: Getting a Kindle book through Libby: borrow in Libby, tap 'Read with Kindle', and Amazon's Send to Kindle page walks you through sending it to your Kindle, or use your browser. Borrow too much? Titles auto-return at the end of the loan period — no overdues, ever.
